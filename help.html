Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?
Choose Register from the header and follow the registration steps. Account creation is shown as a design preview here and is not connected.
How can I update my account details?
Open your account profile and review the details available for editing. For this prototype, profile management is not connected.
How do I deposit using MTN Mobile Money?
Open Wallet, choose Deposit, select MTN Mobile Money, enter the number and amount, then continue. This prototype does not send a payment request.
How do I deposit using Airtel Money?
Open Wallet, choose Deposit, select Airtel Money, enter the number and amount, then continue. This prototype does not connect to Airtel Money.
How do I withdraw funds?
Open Wallet, switch to Withdraw, choose MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money, and enter the registered number and amount. Withdrawal processing is not connected in this design.
Where can I find football and basketball leagues?
Browse football competitions on the Sports page, or open the Basketball page to explore sample competitions by region and country.
Are the fixtures and odds live?
No. Fixture names, schedules, and odds shown in this prototype are sample content. They are not connected to a live sports data provider.
Where do I find virtual games?
Open the Virtual Games or the Casino page from the main navigation. You can browse sample categories such as virtual football, racing, and basketball.
Can I play virtual games here?
No. This is a visual prototype. Game launch and results are not connected.
Where is the Casino page?
Open the Casino page from the site navigation. Its game catalogue is sample content and does not launch games.
How can I keep play within limits?
Set a personal spending and time limit before you start, and step away if play stops feeling comfortable. This prototype does not provide account-level limit controls.
